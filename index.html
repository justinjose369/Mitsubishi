About Us · Distributors over 25 years

Middle East Group is one of Libya’s leading automobile distributors today, became associated with Mitsubishi Motors Corporation for more than 25 years.

Middle East Group offers an unbeaten choice of Mitsubishi passenger cars, SUVs, commercial vehicles.

We are driven by professionalism and quality, offering the highest standards of customer service. This has positioned us as a top Mitsubishi dealer in the North African region performing impressively in individual and fleet sales.

Middle East Group has been synonymous with the auto market in Libya. We fully support the automotive market through a growing network of hi-tech service centers managed by a qualified and experienced team supported with extensive Mitsubishi spare parts.

Much of the Middle East Group’s growth is through our strict adherence to excellence delivered by highly trained staff and continuous investment in showrooms and service facilities guaranteeing customer satisfaction and long-term relationship.
